Falusi CSOK — village family housing support

Government of Hungary

Hungary's Falusi CSOK provides up to ~HUF 15M (~€38k) to buy, build, expand or renovate homes in ~2,500 designated small/peripheral villages; combinable with a 3% subsidised loan.

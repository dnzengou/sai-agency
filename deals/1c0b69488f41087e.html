Our Living Islands — Vacant Property Refurbishment Grant

Dept. of Rural and Community Development and the Gaeltacht · Ireland

Ireland's 10-year offshore-islands policy (2023) supports repopulation via remote-working infrastructure and Vacant Property Refurbishment Grants up to €60,000 (vacant) or €84,000 (derelict) to renovate and occupy pre-1993 island homes.

PRR call 'IA nas PME' - AI adoption grants for SMEs

Recuperar Portugal (PRR) · Portugal

Portugal's Recovery and Resilience Plan made EUR 100M available for SMEs to adopt AI, as a non-repayable subsidy covering 75% of investment up to EUR 300,000 per company; Phase II closed 28 November 2025.
